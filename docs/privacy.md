# Privacy

This page explains what the AirReps guide measures about visits, and what it doesn't.

**Last updated:** October 22, 2026

## Page and link counts

The guide keeps simple daily totals so we can see which pages help readers, roughly where they come from, and which outbound links get used. Each time a page opens, a counter adds one to a total made of:

- **The page**, as its plain address such as `/links/pro`, without anything after `?` or `#`
- **A broad source category**, such as Google search, Bing, an AI assistant (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Perplexity), Reddit/Discord, social media, direct, or other. The category is worked out in your browser, and the referring address itself is never sent to the counter.
- **For outbound clicks**, the destination: a trusted seller, KakoBuy, KakoGuide, Discord, or Reddit

Nothing else goes into the counts. They hold no personal data: no IP address, no browser details, no cookies or device IDs, no search terms or anything you type. They also don't record individual visits or link one page to the next. Daily totals are kept for about 90 days.

A counted click only means someone opened a link. It is not a purchase, and sellers don't share orders with us through it.

## Cloudflare Web Analytics

The guide also loads Cloudflare Web Analytics for page views and loading speed. Its script sends Cloudflare the page address, the referring page, and timing measurements. Cloudflare says Web Analytics doesn't use cookies or local storage and doesn't fingerprint visitors. See Cloudflare's [Web Analytics documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/about/) and [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## No cookies or cross-site identifiers

The guide sets no analytics cookies, stores nothing in your browser for measurement, and has no identifiers that follow you to other sites.

## Opting out

If your browser sends **Do Not Track** or **Global Privacy Control**, the guide loads neither the counter nor Cloudflare Web Analytics. Content blockers work too.

## Server logs

Like most websites, the services that host the guide and the counter may keep ordinary access and security logs, such as IP address, browser type, and time of request. Those logs are separate from the counts above.

## Other sites

Sellers, KakoBuy, Discord and Reddit have their own privacy policies. The AirReps Discord bot is covered by its own [privacy policy](/bot/privacy).
