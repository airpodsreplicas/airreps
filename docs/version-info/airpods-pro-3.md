## V7 Overview {#v7-overview}

V7 is the newest AirPods Pro 3 replica and comes in two builds at the same price ($67–68): the **Huilian** (our main all-round / Apple-ecosystem recommendation) and the **TigerBuilder** (our **Best ANC** pick, with 3-device multipoint and FlyCC). For a side-by-side comparison, see [AirPods Pro 3 V7 Huilian vs V7 TB](/articles/airpods-pro-3-v7-huilian-vs-v7-tb).

### V7 Huilian {#v7-huilian}

The **V7 Huilian** is our main all-round recommendation, based on the Huilian 377H3 chipset ($67–68). It includes a heart rate sensor, head gesture controls for answering calls, AI Hybrid ANC (~38–42 dB), working head-tracking spatial audio, Live Translation, iCloud Connect, a low-latency gaming mode, and the StarFun app (Android & iOS). The 45mAh earbuds are rated around 7 hours of playback, and the 500mAh case delivers 3–4 full charges. It's sold by Earhive, HiCity and Jenny — see the [purchase links](/links/airpods-pro-3).

## SE Overview {#se-overview}

The SE is a Jenny-exclusive Pro 3 replica powered by the **XiaoBai chipset**, positioned at $48 as the standout **best value under $50** in the Pro 3 line.

The headline is value for money: very strong ANC for under $50, getting close to retail. Sound sits around 80% of retail — punchy low end, clear vocals, and very natural transparency mode. Head-tracking spatial audio genuinely works without quirks, and it includes real-time translation, slide volume adjustment, wireless charging, and in-ear detection. Stem controls are snappy, static is essentially non-existent, and build quality is solid with strong case lid magnets. The one physical quirk: the double-tap on the case front to connect needs a firmer press than retail.

Where it trades off for its $48 price point: it connects to one device at a time (no seamless multi-device switching or iCloud Connect), and it does not have conversation awareness or head-gesture call answering (nodding/shaking). While heart rate monitoring is built in, our testing showed it tracks 15–20 bpm off a medical-grade sensor — fine for casual curiosity, but if you want a functional optical sensor, the V7 tracks significantly closer.

**SE vs V7:** the **SE** wins on price ($48 vs $67–68) with very strong ANC for the money; the **V7 TB** takes the top spot for Best ANC overall plus 3-device multipoint and FlyCC; the **V7 Huilian** is our main all-round recommendation for iCloud Connect, Live Translation, accurate optical heart rate tracking, conversation awareness, head gestures, and StarFun app support. On a sub-$50 budget, the SE is the easy recommendation. It's exclusively sold by Jenny — see the [purchase links](/links/airpods-pro-3), and check [Pro 3 SE threads on r/AirReps](https://www.reddit.com/r/AirReps/search?q=%22pro+3+se%22&restrict_sr=1) for community sentiment.
