## Frequently Asked Questions

::: details Which AirPods Pro replica is the best right now?
Our main all-round recommendation is the **AirPods Pro 3 V7 Huilian** ($67–68) for iCloud Connect, Live Translation, heart rate tracking, head gestures, and the StarFun app. Choose the **Pro 3 V7 TB** ($67–68) for the **best ANC** overall plus 3-device multipoint and FlyCC, the Jenny-exclusive **Pro 3 SE** ($48) as the best value under $50, or the **AirPods Pro 2 V5.3 TB** ($55–57) if you want the most mature Pro 2.
:::

::: details Should I buy AirPods Pro 3 V7 Huilian or V7 TB?
Both cost $67–68 and share heart rate tracking, head gestures, and conversation awareness. Pick **V7 Huilian** if you use a Mac or iPad (iCloud Connect), want Live Translation, or want to avoid the FlyCC popup caveat. Pick **V7 TB** if you want the **best ANC**, 3-device multipoint (including non-Apple devices), and FlyCC. See [V7 Huilian vs V7 TB](/articles/airpods-pro-3-v7-huilian-vs-v7-tb).
:::

::: details Which AirPods Pro replica has the best noise cancelling?
The **AirPods Pro 3 V7 TB** ($67–68) is our Best ANC pick overall. Under $50, the Jenny-exclusive **AirPods Pro 3 SE** ($48) offers strong ANC for the price. Among Pro 2 models, the **V5.3 TB** ($55–57) leads with 34.5 dB average and 41 dB peak ANC.
:::

::: details Do AirPods Pro replicas work with Find My or iCloud Connect?
No replica supports Apple's encrypted Find My network. Seamless **iCloud Connect** switching between Apple devices is exclusive to Huilian models: Pro 3 V7 Huilian, Pro 3 V6 Huilian, Pro 2 V5.4 Huilian, and Pro 2 V5.3 Huilian.
