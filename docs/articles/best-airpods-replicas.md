## Quick chipset & manufacturer guide

Version numbers like `V7` and `V5.3` are community standards, while the suffix names the manufacturer and chipset inside:

- **Huilian (377H3, 377, 277, 247):** The only silicon with **iCloud Connect** (exclusive to 5 models: Pro 3 V7 Huilian, Pro 3 V6 Huilian, Pro 2 V5.4 Huilian, Pro 2 V5.3 Huilian, and AirPods 4 V2 Huilian). Best for Mac users; uses **StarFun** (Android & iOS).
- **TigerBuilder / TB (Airoha 1571AM, 1562AE, 1563E):** Known for strong ANC, tough sweat-resistant cases, and 2- or 3-device Bluetooth multipoint. Uses **FlyCC** (Android & iOS).
- **XiaoBai:** Powers the Jenny-exclusive Pro 3 SE with strong value-tier ANC under $50.
- **Bluetrum (8922):** Powers budget entry models like Pro 3 V5.5 with basic ANC.
- **Jieli (JL):** Ultra-cheap clones found on random marketplaces — no community app support, poor ANC, avoid.
