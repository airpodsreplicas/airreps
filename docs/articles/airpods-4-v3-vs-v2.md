## What all three AirPods 4 versions share

Every current AirPods 4 replica from verified sellers includes:

- Open-fit (non-in-ear) AirPods 4 design with Force Touch stem controls (like retail AirPods 4, there is no stem volume-swipe sensor)
- USB-C charging case with a case speaker
- In-ear detection, Hey Siri, iOS pairing popup, About page, and iOS 27 support
- No Apple Find My network (precision finding is missing on every replica; see [Do Fake AirPods Work With Find My?](/articles/do-fake-airpods-work-with-find-my))

Keep expectations realistic on noise cancelling: because open-fit earbuds do not seal your ear canal with silicone tips, open-ear ANC naturally blocks less low-frequency rumble than in-ear [AirPods Pro replicas](/version-info/pro).

## FAQ

::: details Is the AirPods 4 V3 TB worth the extra cost over the V2 TB?
Yes if you use ANC, head-tracking spatial audio, conversation awareness, or switch between 3 devices. The V3 TB upgrades from the Airoha 1563E to the 1571AM with dual-mic hybrid ANC and real head tracking. If you only want basic open-ear earbuds around $30, the V2 TB is plenty.
:::

::: details Does the AirPods 4 V3 TB have iCloud Connect?
No. The AirPods 4 V3 TB uses the Airoha 1571AM chipset with 3-device Bluetooth multipoint, not iCloud Connect. If you want iCloud Connect on an AirPods 4 replica, buy the **AirPods 4 V2 Huilian** ($39–42).
:::

::: details Do AirPods 4 replicas have stem volume swipe controls?
No. Just like genuine AirPods 4, AirPods 4 replicas use Force Touch pinch controls on the stem rather than capacitive volume swipes. Stem slide volume control is an AirPods Pro feature.
:::

::: details How does AirPods 4 replica ANC compare to AirPods Pro replicas?
Because AirPods 4 sit open in the ear without silicone tips, their ANC reduces background hum noticeably less than sealed in-ear models like the AirPods Pro 3 SE, Pro 3 V7, or Pro 2 V5.3 TB. Choose AirPods 4 for open-ear comfort; choose AirPods Pro for maximum noise isolation.
:::
