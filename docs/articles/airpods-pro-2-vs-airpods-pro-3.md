# AirPods Pro 2 vs AirPods Pro 3 Replicas: Which Should You Buy?

With the release of AirPods Pro 3 replicas (specifically the **V7 Huilian**, **SE**, and **V6**), buyers face an important mid-funnel decision: should you buy the newly released Pro 3 clone, or stick with the proven, highly refined **AirPods Pro 2 replica** (the V5.4 Huilian or V5.3 TB)?

Both families are strong buys. The Pro 2 line has had years of batch revisions, while the Pro 3 line brings the newest form factor and features. Here is the honest head-to-head comparison from community testing.

> **Quick Answer:** If you want the newest Pro 3 features with iCloud Connect and the iOS popup, get the **AirPods Pro 3 V7 Huilian** (~$67): the Pro 3 form factor, AI Hybrid ANC, an optical heart rate sensor, head gestures, Live Translation, and a low-latency gaming mode. The **AirPods Pro 2 V5.4 Huilian** (~$58) is still an excellent all-rounder with stable ~38dB ANC, adaptive audio, and iCloud switching for a little less, and the Jenny-exclusive **Pro 3 SE** (~$48) is the value pick with the strongest ANC.

## 1. Active Noise Cancellation & Transparency

Both families deliver strong noise cancellation:

- **AirPods Pro 2 (V5.4 & V5.3 TB)**: These models represent the acoustic ceiling of replica hardware. Community laboratory sound measurements benchmark the Pro 2 V5.3 TB at an average of 34.5 dB reduction (peaking at 41 dB in lower frequencies), providing near-retail silence on public transit. The V5.4 Huilian offers slightly smoother transparency mode transitions with zero background static.
- **AirPods Pro 3 (V7 & SE)**: The Pro 3 SE is the pleasant surprise of the line, delivering punchy passive isolation and strong ANC at an accessible price. The flagship V7 uses dual-feed AI Hybrid ANC, rated around 38–42 dB on the [version page](/version-info/airpods-pro-3), and the Huilian build pairs it with iCloud Connect.

## 2. Unique Pro 3 features: gimmick vs useful?

The Pro 3 clones introduce several new hardware capabilities:

- **Heart Rate Sensor**: Present on the V7 and SE. On the SE, the sensor is inaccurate (tracks 15–20 bpm off) and should be treated as casual curiosity. On the V7, readings are more responsive via the optical sensor, but still lag behind dedicated fitness trackers.
- **Head Gesture Controls**: The V7 allows you to nod your head to accept incoming phone calls or shake your head to decline. This functions surprisingly well in practice.
- **Live Translation**: Present on the Pro 3 V7, V6 Huilian, and SE. They trigger iOS translation prompt popups or companion app audio pass-through, giving quick access to conversational audio translation.

## 3. Apple Ecosystem & iCloud Connect

If you use multiple Apple devices (such as an iPhone alongside a MacBook or iPad), device switching is critical:

- **AirPods Pro 2 V5.4 Huilian** features reliable **iCloud Connect**: opening the case on an iPhone registers the earbuds across your iCloud account, enabling smooth manual switching on your Mac or iPad without re-pairing.
- **AirPods Pro 3 SE** lacks native iCloud synchronization entirely; you must manually disconnect and reconnect via Bluetooth settings when switching between an iPhone and a laptop. If seamless switching is important, you must choose either the Pro 2 V5.4 or the Pro 3 V6/V7 Huilian.

## 4. Build quality and ergonomics

Both families use high-grade ABS plastic with weighted zinc-alloy lid hinges and clean seam tolerances. 

The primary difference is the physical earbud contour: the Pro 3 features a subtly slimmer acoustic nozzle and revised angle intended to seat deeper in the ear canal. If you find standard AirPods Pro tips fatigue your ears over long listening sessions, the Pro 3 housing may provide a more natural seal.
