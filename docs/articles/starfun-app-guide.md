# StarFun APK Guide: EQ & Firmware for Huilian AirPods Replicas

StarFun is the companion app for AirPods replicas built on **Huilian** chipsets (the 247, 277, 377 and 377H3). It does for Huilian models what [FlyCC](/articles/flycc-app-guide) does for Airoha and TigerBuilder models: you use it to adjust the EQ and to update the firmware. If you own a Huilian replica such as the AirPods Pro 3 V7 Huilian or the Pro 2 V5.4, StarFun is the app to install.

> **Quick Answer:** StarFun is the app for Huilian-based AirPods replicas: the AirPods Pro 3 V7 Huilian and V6 Huilian, the AirPods Pro 2 V5.4 and V5.3 Huilian, and the AirPods 4 V2 Huilian. Get it as an Android APK or from the iOS App Store via [Useful Apps](/useful-apps#huilian). Use it to adjust EQ and, only when something is broken, to update firmware. TigerBuilder models use FlyCC instead. If no app connects to your earbuds, they're probably a low-tier Jieli clone.

The EQ can vary by seller, so the same version can sound different from one batch to the next. ## Update the firmware with StarFun

Start with the community's cardinal rule: **if it works, don't touch it.** Firmware updates are released mainly to fix breakage from major iOS updates or serious hardware bugs. If your earbuds connect smoothly and sound right, updating only adds the risk of bricking them.

If you do need an update, go through the safety checklist first:

1. Charge both earbuds and the case above 80%.
2. Keep the case connected to power during the update.
3. Leave both earbuds seated in the case with the lid open.
4. Keep your phone's screen awake so the transfer doesn't stall.

Then update in StarFun:

1. Make sure the earbuds are connected to your phone and the lid is open.
2. Open **StarFun** and tap the **Settings / Firmware** icon.
3. Check the current firmware version against the latest cloud release.
4. Tap **Upgrade**. The app writes the configuration blocks one after another.
5. After the earbuds reboot, forget them in your phone's Bluetooth menu and do a soft reset.

Only use StarFun on Huilian earbuds. Flashing firmware meant for a different chipset can brick the earbuds, and firmware meant for an Airoha chip instantly bricks a Huilian board.

If the update freezes at a percentage for more than 5 minutes, don't close the lid or take the earbuds out. Retry or restart the app with the earbuds still in place. If the earbuds stop responding completely, follow the hard reset in [How to Reset Fake AirPods](/articles/how-to-reset-fake-airpods), then reopen StarFun. In most cases the update can be flashed again from 0%. The [firmware update guide](/articles/how-to-update-fake-airpods-firmware) covers every chipset.

## StarFun won't find your earbuds?

Work through these in order:

- **Pair them in Bluetooth settings first.** StarFun needs the earbuds already connected to your phone.
- **Check the chip.** StarFun is for Huilian models. TigerBuilder (Airoha) models use FlyCC, and HR models use BullSuper. The standard way to identify your chip is to try the apps on [Useful Apps](/useful-apps) one at a time and see which one connects.
- **No app connects at all?** Then your pair is probably a very low-tier clone on a Jieli chipset, which none of the community apps support. Genuine Apple AirPods won't respond to these apps either.
- **Connection keeps dropping?** Clear the pairing cache with a controller reset from the [reset guide](/articles/how-to-reset-fake-airpods). For pairing problems in general, see [Fake AirPods Won't Connect](/articles/fake-airpods-wont-connect).
