# AirPods Pro 3 V7 Huilian vs V7 TB: Which V7 Replica to Buy?

The V7 is the newest AirPods Pro 3 replica, and it comes in two builds from two manufacturers. The **V7 Huilian** is the original V7, built on the Huilian 377H3 chipset. The **V7 TigerBuilder** (TB) is the newest V7 build, based on the Airoha 1571AM. Both cost the same, both are sold by Earhive, HiCity and Jenny, and most of the feature list is shared. They differ in how they switch between devices, live translation, the companion app, and what happens after a firmware update.

> **Quick Answer:** Both V7 builds cost $67–68 and share heart rate, head gestures, conversation awareness, head-tracking spatial audio and very strong noise cancelling (~38–42 dB). The **V7 TB** is the newest and best V7 build, with multipoint for up to 3 devices and the FlyCC app, but after a firmware update in FlyCC its iOS popup and serial number disappear and have to be re-added. Pick the **V7 Huilian** if you use an iPhone with a Mac or iPad, or want live translation: it has iCloud Connect and the StarFun app, and the guide recommends Huilian models for Mac.

## What both V7 builds share

You get the same core Pro 3 hardware with either build:

- **Heart rate sensor.** Both V7 builds have one. The V7's optical sensor tracks much closer than the SE's, but readings still lag behind dedicated fitness trackers.
- **Head gestures.** Nod to accept an incoming call, shake your head to decline.
- **Conversation awareness and head-tracking spatial audio** on both.
- **Very strong noise cancelling**, rated around 38–42 dB on both builds.
- **The Pro 3 basics** every version has: USB-C charging, stem slide volume control, Active Noise Cancelling, Transparency Mode, in-ear detection, the Pro 3 iOS popup and iOS 27 support.

Neither build works with Apple's Find My network. No replica does; see [Do Fake AirPods Work With Find My?](/articles/do-fake-airpods-work-with-find-my)

## Difference 1: switching between devices

This is the main reason to pick one build over the other.

- **V7 Huilian: iCloud Connect.** iCloud Connect is exclusive to Huilian-based models. It syncs the earbuds across Apple devices logged into the same Apple ID, so after pairing with your iPhone they show up on your Mac or iPad without pairing again.
- **V7 TB: multipoint for 3 devices.** Airoha chipsets such as the 1571AM use standard Bluetooth Multipoint instead. The TB connects to up to 3 devices without Apple ID integration. To move it from your phone to your Mac, you click **Connect** in the Mac's Bluetooth menu.

iCloud Connect only links Apple devices. If your devices aren't all Apple (an iPhone and a Windows laptop, for example), the TB's 3-device multipoint is the switching you'll actually use.

## Difference 2: using them with a Mac

The guide recommends Huilian models if you plan to use your replicas with a MacBook or iMac. Other clones lack iCloud switching, or have their volume stuck at 100% on macOS. That second problem affects TB models. A free third-party app partly fixes it, but on TB models the volume gestures on the earbuds won't work with that fix. See the [macOS volume slider fix](/troubleshooting/macOS-volume-slider-fixup) and [Do Fake AirPods Work With Mac?](/articles/fake-airpods-on-mac)

If a Mac is part of your daily setup, the V7 Huilian is the simpler choice.

## Difference 3: live translation

The V7 Huilian supports live translation. The V7 TB doesn't. If real-time translation is on your list, that decides it.

## Difference 4: the app and firmware updates

- **V7 Huilian → StarFun.** Huilian chipsets, including the 377H3, use StarFun for EQ and firmware updates. It's on Android and the iOS App Store. See the [StarFun guide](/articles/starfun-app-guide).
- **V7 TB → FlyCC.** TigerBuilder models use FlyCC, which is also on Android and the iOS App Store. See the [FlyCC guide](/articles/flycc-app-guide).

The TB comes with the iOS popup and a serial number. After a firmware update in the FlyCC app, both disappear and need to be re-added, which you do in the FlyCC app. That isn't for everyone. The V7 Huilian isn't affected.

Either way, the community's firmware rule applies: if your earbuds work well, don't update. Updates are released mainly to fix breakage from major iOS updates or serious hardware bugs. The [firmware update guide](/articles/how-to-update-fake-airpods-firmware) covers the safe procedure for both apps.

## Difference 5: listed hardware

The guide lists different hardware details for each build:

- **V7 Huilian:** AI Hybrid ANC and a low-latency gaming mode. The 45mAh earbuds are rated around 7 hours of playback, and the 500mAh case gives 3–4 full charges.
- **V7 TB (seller-listed):** adaptive ANC, swipe volume control, dual-mic ENC, a 10mm driver, 45mAh earbuds with a 550mAh Qi case, and a case speaker.

The TB figures come from the sellers and the Huilian's runtime is a rating, so don't read a battery difference into the case sizes.
