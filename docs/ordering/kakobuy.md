## Why Use an Agent?

- **QC Photos** — KakoBuy photographs your items before shipping so you can verify quality
- **Card-friendly checkout** — pay the agent by card or PayPal instead of a seller's own payment gateway; the methods offered and any verification step depend on your country and payment provider
- **Combine Shipping** — Bundle multiple items into one parcel to save on shipping costs
- **Return/exchange requests** — if QC shows a problem, you can ask KakoBuy to request a return or exchange from the seller before international shipping; acceptance, deadlines, and any fees depend on the seller and KakoBuy's terms

:::

## How to Order

### 1. Create an Account

Sign up at [KakoBuy](https://airreps.link/kakobuy). You only need an email address to get started. New accounts receive a welcome pack of shipping coupons — [KakoGuide's discount code page](https://kakoguide.com/guides/kakobuy-discount-codes) keeps track of what's currently active and how to redeem it.

![KakoBuy signup page](/images/kakobuy/signup.webp)

### 2. Find the Product

Go to our [Links](/links/info) section and choose the product you want. Each seller's page has a **KakoBuy** button that takes you directly to the product on KakoBuy — no need to copy and paste links manually.

![AirReps link chooser showing Weidian and KakoBuy options](/images/kakobuy/link-chooser.webp)

Alternatively, you can copy any **Weidian** link and paste it into the KakoBuy search bar at the top of the page.

### 3. Select Options & Add to Cart

On the KakoBuy product page, you'll see the product details pulled from the seller's Weidian store — including price, available models/versions, and reference photos. Select the correct model (e.g. Pro3-V6-TB, V5.3TB) and click **Add to Shopping Cart** or **Buy now**.

![KakoBuy product page showing HiCity AirPods with model selection](/images/kakobuy/product-page.webp)

### 4. Pay for Your Order

Check out and pay using one of the methods shown at checkout — for example credit/debit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or balance top-up. KakoBuy accepts multiple currencies. The methods offered depend on your country, and the payment provider or your card issuer may run its own security or identity checks on some payments.

::: details How long does this take?
KakoBuy usually reviews your order within 3–5 hours, then places it with the seller. The seller typically ships to KakoBuy's warehouse within 3–5 days.
:::

### 5. Review QC Photos

Once the item arrives at KakoBuy's warehouse, they'll take quality control (QC) photos. Check these in **User Center** → **Order**, where you'll see the QC pictures attached to each item. If something looks off, you can ask KakoBuy to request a return or exchange from the seller before shipping internationally — whether the seller accepts, the deadline, and any return shipping or fees depend on the seller and KakoBuy's terms.

![KakoBuy orders page with QC photos](/images/kakobuy/orders-qc.webp)

::: tip Inspect before shipping
This is a great perk of using an agent — you get to inspect your AirPods replica before committing to international shipping. Check the [QC & LC guide](/ordering/qc-lc) for tips on what to look for.
:::

### 6. Ship Internationally

When you're happy with the QC photos, submit a shipping request:

1. Go to **User Center** → **Warehouse**
2. Select the items you want to ship
3. Fill in your shipping address and choose a shipping method
4. Pay the international shipping fee

AirPods contain batteries, so choose a line that accepts battery items for your destination — restrictions vary by line and country, so check the line's details before you submit. Delivery commonly takes around **7–25 days** depending on the line, customs, and your location; that's an estimate, not a guarantee. You can combine multiple items into one parcel to save on shipping costs. Optional [Kako Insurance](https://item.kakobuy.com/service/help/question?id=123) is only offered on some lines and has to be added when you submit the parcel; each line's compensation limits and exclusions apply.

### 7. Track Your Package

After shipment, track your parcel under **User Center** → **Parcel**. You'll see the shipping method, tracking number, and live status updates as the package moves through international transit.

::: details Will I be charged customs fees or VAT on a KakoBuy order?
It depends on your destination, the shipping line, and the parcel. Import VAT, customs duty, and handling or carrier fees are separate charges set by your country's rules; some lines include certain charges in the shipping price, while on others they can be collected on delivery. Always use the real purchase value and an accurate description of the goods — don't ask for a lower declared value — and check what your selected line's quote actually includes (VAT, duty, handling fees, insurance). A route name such as "Triangle" or "tax-included" isn't a guarantee on its own.

**EU (checked 22 October 2026):** the €150 customs-duty exemption for low-value parcels ended on 1 July 2026. Qualifying distance-sale parcels up to €150 are subject to a [temporary €3 customs duty](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/053e5b4e-f0be-4f20-9a23-3e3b659a6676_en?filename=Customs+Guidance+on+EUR+3+customs+duty.pdf) per distinct tariff category in the parcel (a declaration line distinguished by classification, description and origin, not each physical piece), with VAT charged separately, and the European Commission has [announced](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/eu-customs-reform_en) a separate €2 Union handling fee, also counted per tariff category, from 1 November 2026. KakoBuy [states](https://www.kakobuy.com/tipdetail?id=52) that its EU Duty-Free Shipping Lines include applicable taxes and duties and that it covers additional customs duties — that's KakoBuy's policy for those lines, not a legal exemption, and it doesn't mention the new handling fee, so confirm what your current quote covers. [KakoBuy shipping & VAT explained](https://kakoguide.com/guides/kakobuy-shipping-vat-explained) covers the details per region.
:::
